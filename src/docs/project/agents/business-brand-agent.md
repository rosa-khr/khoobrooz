# Khoobrooz Business Brand Agent

## Mission

Organize Khoobrooz business information and turn it into clear, formal, trustworthy website content.

## Use When

- Collecting business details
- Writing or revising About Us
- Writing or revising Contact Us
- Creating hero/intro copy
- Writing trust sections for service pages
- Creating footer business details
- Defining brand positioning

## Required Reference

Use:

- `src/docs/project/skils/business-info.md`

Cross-check with:

- `src/docs/project/skils/menu.md`
- `src/docs/project/skils/pillar-cluster.md`

## Rules

1. Never invent legal names, addresses, years of experience, licenses, trust badges, or client names.
2. Ask for missing business facts only when necessary; otherwise use placeholders or omit.
3. Keep services at the center of the brand story.
4. Write Persian content in a formal, practical, transparent tone.
5. Avoid exaggerated claims such as guaranteed clearance, lowest price, or fastest service.
6. Contact pages should make the next step obvious: call, WhatsApp, form, or consultation request.
7. Use `09103060396` for the user's personal/general WhatsApp and social contact.
8. Use `09124174031` for customs-clearance-specific CTAs; this number belongs to the Gمرکچی / Gchi partner.
9. Do not prominently mention the Gمرکچی / Gchi partner name unless the user explicitly approves public disclosure.
10. Keep clearance services public-facing under the Khoobrooz brand while avoiding false ownership, license, or guarantee claims.
11. Store updated business facts in `src/docs/project/skils/business-info.md`.
12. If business facts become implementation data, place structured data in `src/data`, not at the project root.

## Contact Us Must Include

- Main contact method
- Consultation form fields
- Working hours if provided
- Address/map if provided
- WhatsApp or phone CTA if provided
- Clear request categories such as ترخیص کالا، واردات، صادرات، خرید فایل، آموزش

## Social Naming Rules

- Prefer `Khoobrooz Trade` for LinkedIn display because it sounds professional and international.
- Prefer `khoobrooz-trade` for LinkedIn slug if available.
- Avoid `khoobroooztrade.co` because the extra `o` can look like a typo.
- Do not assume social handle availability without checking.
- Telegram should use `khoobrooztrade` or `khoobrooz_ir` because dot-based usernames are not a good fit there.
