# 01 Business Brand Agent Run

## Output Goal

Prepare the business and brand information needed before writing Khoobrooz About Us, Contact Us, footer, trust sections, and homepage intro copy.

## Current Brand Position

Khoobrooz is a formal, service-first trade brand focused on:

- ترخیص کالا
- واردات و ثبت سفارش
- واردات از چین و کارگو
- صادرات کالا
- آموزش صادرات و واردات
- فروش فایل‌ها و اسناد تجاری

Khoobrooz should not be presented only as an academy or only as a digital document shop. The main identity is a practical trade services brand, with education and files supporting trust, SEO, and revenue.

## Social Identity Recommendation

Recommended:

- LinkedIn display name: Khoobrooz Trade
- LinkedIn slug: `khoobrooz-trade`
- Instagram: `khoobrooz.ir` or `khoobrooztrade`
- Telegram: `khoobrooztrade` or `khoobrooz_ir`

Avoid:

- `khoobroooztrade.co`

Reason:

- The extra `o` can look like a typo.
- `.co` is less clear unless it is the final official domain.
- LinkedIn should feel like a company/brand name, not only a domain.
- Telegram does not fit dot-based domain-style usernames well.

CTA routing:

- Use `09103060306` anywhere the CTA is specifically for ترخیص کالا or customs clearance.
- Use `09103060306` for the user's personal/general WhatsApp and social contact.

## Trust Rules

Use only real, user-approved proof points. Do not invent experience years, licenses, addresses, client names, or guarantees.

Avoid:

- تضمین قطعی ترخیص
- سریع‌ترین ترخیص
- کمترین هزینه بازار
- نتیجه قطعی گمرکی یا حقوقی
